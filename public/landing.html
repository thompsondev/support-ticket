Everything your server needs

Per-guild isolation — every server gets its own config, types, links and data.

🎫

Full ticket system

Panels with dropdown or buttons, modal intake forms, private channels, claiming, priorities, notes, ticket types per server.

📄

Transcripts & ratings

Self-contained HTML transcripts on close, posted to a log channel, plus a 1–5 star DM rating flow with feedback.

🔨

Moderation suite

Warn / kick / ban / timeout with persistent warning history, DM notifications and a complete event log channel.

🛡️

Native AutoMod

Keyword blocklists, spam detection, mass-mention limits and invite-link blocking — managed via /automod.

🎉

Giveaways & polls

Timed giveaways with live entry counts and winner rolls; button-vote polls with auto-close and winner announcements.

💡

Suggestions box

Member suggestions with 👍/👎 voting and a staff status workflow — pending → approved → implemented.

⚡

Godmode

Bot owners get fleet-wide control: global blacklist, per-server kill switch, force-leave, and stats across every guild.

🖥️

Web dashboard

Discord OAuth2 login — configure ticket types, roles, links, welcome messages and more without touching Discord.

🧩

And more…

Reaction roles, sticky messages, AFK status, reminders, welcome + autorole, audit logging, canned replies, announcements.
